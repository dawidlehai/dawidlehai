# 🎓 My course projects

A showcase of my course projects, which were part of my learning journey in web development. Each project demonstrates different skills and technologies I've learned.

- [Remix Expenses](https://github.com/dawidlehai/remix-expenses) - Create an account to add, edit, remove and manage expenses with simple analytics.

- [Delifood](https://github.com/dawidlehai/delifood) - Demo food order app made with React. Add and remove products from the cart and submit a fake order.

- [React Expenses Tracker](https://github.com/dawidlehai/react-expenses-tracker) - Simple demo expenses tracker made in React. Add your expenses and sort them by year.

- [Forkify](https://github.com/dawidlehai/forkify) - Search for recipes, bookmark them, or upload your own.

- [Omnifood](https://github.com/dawidlehai/omnifood) - Home page for a fictional AI-powered food subscription service.

- [Nexter](https://github.com/dawidlehai/nexter) - Demo landing page for a fictional company that sells luxury homes.

- [Trillo](https://github.com/dawidlehai/trillo) - Demo hotel booking service.

- [Mapty](https://github.com/dawidlehai/mapty) - Running and cycling workout tracker with a map.

- [Bankist](https://github.com/dawidlehai/bankist) - Demo banking app. Sort transactions, transfer money and more.
